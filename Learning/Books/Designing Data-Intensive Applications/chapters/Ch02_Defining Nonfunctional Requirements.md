## Flashcards
What is the difference between a fault and a failure?::A fault is when one component deviates from its spec; a failure is when the system as a whole stops providing the required service to users.
Why are percentiles (p50, p99) more useful than averages for describing latency?::Percentiles show the distribution of response times experienced by real users, while averages can hide long tail latencies that affect a significant minority.
What does "operability" mean as a maintainability concern?::Making the system easy for operations teams to keep running smoothly — good monitoring, automation, documentation, and predictable behavior.
