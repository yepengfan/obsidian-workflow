## Flashcards
When would you choose a document model over a relational model?::When your data has a tree-like, self-contained structure (one-to-many relationships), schema flexibility is needed, and you rarely need many-to-many joins.
What is the key advantage of a graph data model?::It excels at representing and querying highly interconnected data with many-to-many relationships, where the connections between entities are as important as the entities themselves.
What distinguishes declarative query languages (like SQL) from imperative ones?::Declarative languages specify *what* data you want, not *how* to get it, allowing the query optimizer to choose the most efficient execution strategy.
