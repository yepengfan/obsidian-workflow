## Flashcards
Why does the book end with a chapter on ethics rather than more technical content?::Because building powerful data systems creates real societal consequences — privacy violations, discrimination, surveillance — and engineers share responsibility for how their systems affect people.
What is the distinction between data as "exhaust" and data as "surveillance"?::Data collected as a byproduct of a service (exhaust) can become surveillance when aggregated and used for purposes users never intended or consented to.
What does the book suggest as a guiding principle for ethical data system design?::Design systems that treat people with respect and dignity, give users agency over their data, and consider the worst-case uses of the systems you build — not just the intended ones.
