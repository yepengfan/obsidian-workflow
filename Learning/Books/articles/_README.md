## AI permission boundary

- **Read**: ✅
- **Annotate / critique**: ✅ (flag logical gaps, technical errors, structural issues)
- **Provide factual corrections and directional hints**: ✅
- **Write prose**: ❌ Never. No paragraphs, no transitions, no polishing.

The human writes every word here. AI reviews after the human finishes.
